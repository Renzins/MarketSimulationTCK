Vanessa Wind Park — Market Strategy Backtester

14 × 4.2 MW (58.8 MW) onshore wind park at Targale, Latvia. 15-minute ISP backtester. Pick the data sources (what counts as "actual" power, what counts as the intra-day forecast) and the strategies to enable — the optimiser then sweeps every checked strategy jointly. Data covers ….

Setup

Define the experiment environment — what data is fed into the simulation and how it is bounded. Setup is not varied by the optimiser and not touched by "Reset to naïve".

DA withhold on low prices optimised

When DA clears at or below price threshold X, hold back fraction Y of the forecast from DA and offer it to balancing instead. Above X, sell the full forecast to DA as usual.

mFRR ↔ aFRR universal offer split optimised

Per-direction routing of balancing volume between mFRR and aFRR. Independent ratios for upward (s_up) and downward (s_dn) because the two markets price each direction differently.

ID trust coefficient optimised

How much of the intra-day forecast revision (ID − DA) to act on as additional mFRR-up volume. Negative revisions are not acted on (no buy-back modelled). Only meaningful when the ID forecast source in Setup is set to "Real intra-day forecast" — otherwise ID − DA = 0 and the term collapses.

Intra-day oversell optimised

S3: for each ISP, if da_sold < "Skip if DA sold ≥" AND (VWAP1H − rolling-mean p_mfrr) ≥ S_min AND recent σ ≤ σ_max, sell up to X_cap MW on intraday and submit a hedge mFRR-dn bid at VWAP1H + M — a stop-loss mFRR-dn offer the park wouldn't normally place (pitched above the typical clearing). The hedge clears whenever p_mfrr ≤ VWAP1H + M, bounding the loss on the oversold MW. Rolling stats span [target − K − L, target − L) — the publication lag L (default 4 ISPs ≈ 1 h) models the fact that an intra-day trader cannot see settlement prices of the ISPs immediately preceding the target. The Optimise button sweeps K, S_min, σ_max and M jointly with the other checked strategies' params — X_cap, L and DA-skip stay at their user-set values.
