Setup

Define the experiment environment — what data is fed into the simulation and how it is bounded. Setup is not varied by the optimiser and not touched by "Reset to naïve".

Reserve market — up capacity optimised

Capacity market for upward reserve, settled before day-ahead. Unlike down capacity it is not free: each awarded MW is held back from DA as ramp-up headroom, so DA and down capacity can only use the remaining forecast (F − awarded). We are paid for availability (price × MW × 0.25 h) and the awarded MW become an obligatory mFRR/aFRR-up offer regardless of wind — if up-activated they enter the position, so a low-wind ISP can fall short and pay imbalance. Only offered once the forecast reaches "Min MW". Off ⇒ no up capacity is offered.

Reserve market — down capacity optimised

Capacity market, settled before day-ahead. When we win down-capacity we are paid for availability (price × MW × 0.25 h) and the awarded MW become mandatory: sold to DA regardless of the clearing price (they bypass the withhold rule) and offered as mFRR/aFRR-down. Activation still uses our own price gates. Only down capacity is modelled. Off ⇒ the reserve market is ignored.

DA withhold on low prices (non-reserve volume) optimised

When DA clears at or below price threshold X, hold back fraction Y of the forecast from DA and offer it to balancing instead. Above X, sell the full forecast to DA as usual.

mFRR ↔ aFRR adaptive offer split optimised

Per-direction routing of balancing volume between mFRR and aFRR that adapts over time. Each direction starts at a split x, and every y ISPs it re-checks which market paid more per MW over the last y ISPs and shifts the split toward the winner by step z. Up and down are independent (x₁/y₁/z₁ and x₂/y₂/z₂). Set z = 0 for a fixed (static) split at x.

ID trust coefficient optimised

How much of the intra-day forecast revision (ID − DA) to act on as additional mFRR-up volume. Negative revisions are not acted on (no buy-back modelled). Only meaningful when the ID forecast source in Setup is set to "Real intra-day forecast" — otherwise ID − DA = 0 and the term collapses.

Intra-day oversell optimised

S3: for each ISP, if da_sold < "Skip if DA sold ≥" AND (VWAP1H − rolling-mean p_mfrr) ≥ S_min AND recent σ ≤ σ_max, sell up to X_cap MW on intraday and submit a hedge mFRR-dn bid at VWAP1H + M — a stop-loss mFRR-dn offer the park wouldn't normally place (pitched above the typical clearing). The hedge clears whenever p_mfrr ≤ VWAP1H + M, bounding the loss on the oversold MW. Rolling stats span [target − K − L, target − L) — the publication lag L (default 4 ISPs ≈ 1 h) models the fact that an intra-day trader cannot see settlement prices of the ISPs immediately preceding the target. The Optimise button sweeps K, S_min, σ_max and M jointly with the other checked strategies' params — X_cap, L and DA-skip stay at their user-set values.
